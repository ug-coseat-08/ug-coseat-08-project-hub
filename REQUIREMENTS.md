## Register rules

- Do not treat an interpretation as confirmed client scope.
- Link requirements to tasks, decisions, risks, pull requests, and evidence.
- Update acceptance criteria before implementation where practical.
- Record who confirmed a requirement and when.
- Keep cancelled or superseded requirements for traceability rather than silently deleting them.

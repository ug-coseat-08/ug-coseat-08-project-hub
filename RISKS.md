# Risk Register

Probability and impact are preliminary until the team completes client discovery.

| ID | Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation | Owner | Trigger | Contingency | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RISK-001 | Client scope and priorities remain unclear. | High | High | Hold the first client meeting early; document scope, priorities, exclusions, and acceptance expectations. | Project team | Work begins without confirmed priorities. | Limit work to reversible discovery and escalate decisions to the client and supervisor. | Open |
| RISK-002 | The team is expected to complete more existing applications than the semester can support. | Medium | High | Inventory applications and estimate work before committing; agree the minimum alpha scope. | Project team | Multiple incomplete applications are declared mandatory. | Propose a prioritised, evidence-based scope and record deferred work. | Open |
| RISK-003 | Repository or environment access is delayed. | Medium | High | Request the application inventory, owners, permissions, setup documentation, and access dependencies at the first client meeting. | Client-contact owner—TBD | Access is not available when discovery begins. | Continue requirements, architecture, test-planning, and research preparation while escalating the access blocker. | Open |
| RISK-004 | Roles are assigned before the necessary work is understood. | Medium | Medium | Defer permanent allocation until after discovery and review allocation regularly. | Project team | Members commit to fixed workstreams before scope confirmation. | Reallocate based on evidence, availability, and project priorities. | Open |
| RISK-005 | Individual contribution evidence is incomplete. | Medium | High | Link tasks, branches, pull requests, reviews, meeting actions, and demonstrations to named members. | Every team member | Weekly work cannot be demonstrated or linked to its owner. | Reconstruct evidence promptly from repository and meeting history; discuss gaps with the supervisor. | Open |
| RISK-006 | GitHub and OneDrive copies diverge. | Medium | Medium | Agree one source of truth and a repeatable mirroring owner/process. | Owner—TBD | Conflicting versions appear in both locations. | Reconcile against approved meeting and Git history, then publish a dated authoritative copy. | Open |
| RISK-007 | Client material, credentials, recordings, or private data are committed accidentally. | Low | High | Keep raw recordings and transcripts untracked; use secret scanning and review changes before pushing. | Entire team | A sensitive file appears in a staged change or pull request. | Stop publication, remove the material safely, rotate exposed credentials if necessary, and notify the appropriate owner. | Open |

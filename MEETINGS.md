## Meeting record standard

Each supervisor, client, and internal meeting record should include:

- Date, time, location or channel, and attendees.
- Agenda and preparation material.
- Evidence or demonstrations reviewed.
- Discussion summary.
- Decisions and their approvers.
- Actions, owners, and deadlines.
- Blockers and unresolved questions.
- Links to relevant requirements, risks, tasks, or pull requests.

Minutes should be circulated promptly for attendee corrections. Raw recordings and transcripts remain local unless every necessary participant and data owner has approved their storage and sharing.

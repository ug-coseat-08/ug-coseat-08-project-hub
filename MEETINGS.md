## Meeting record standard

Use one concise, consistent template for supervisor, client, and internal meetings. Each record should include:

- Date, time, channel, and meeting type.
- Every attendee by name and every absent member by name (`None` if nobody was absent).
- Agenda and important discussion only.
- Decisions or agreements.
- Actions, owners, and deadlines.

File each week's minutes by Sunday in the relevant `supervisor`, `group`, or `client` folder. GitHub is the complete shared record; raw recordings and transcripts remain local unless every necessary participant and data owner has approved their storage and sharing.

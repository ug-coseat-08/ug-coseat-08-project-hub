## Purpose

Introduce the unit workflow, explain expectations for weekly supervision and participation, and identify the team's immediate client-engagement and documentation tasks.

## Discussion summary

- Weeks 1–6 are the preparation and sprint-planning phase. The team will clarify client expectations, define user stories, prepare the Jira board, and decide the initial priorities.
- The delivery phase comprises two three-week sprints: approximately weeks 7–9 and weeks 10–12. Each sprint ends with a client review and demonstration. The expected semester outcome is an alpha release whose scope is agreed with the client.
- Assessment activity occurs around weeks 14–15; the supervisor will explain the relevant assessments and templates progressively.
- From the next supervisor meeting, every member must use a computer, turn on their camera when requested, share their screen, and give an approximately five-minute account of work completed during the preceding seven days.
- Weekly attendance, participation, and individual contribution are recorded and may affect individual marks. Members should notify the supervisor if an emergency prevents attendance.
- The supervisor is available by email or Microsoft Teams for questions. Draft assessment work may be shown during meetings for feedback before submission.
- Every supervisor, group, and client meeting must have concise minutes recording the date, attendees, discussion, and plan before the next meeting. GitHub is an acceptable document repository; OneDrive may also be used.

## Decisions and agreements

1. The team will hold a one-hour internal group meeting every week.
2. The team will document one set of supervisor minutes and one set of group minutes each week, plus minutes for every client meeting.
3. The first client contact must be professional, include proposed meeting times, and invite the client to suggest alternatives if none are suitable.
4. The first client meeting should be arranged early in the following week where the client's availability permits.
5. One team member will open the first client meeting, introduce the team, and begin the prepared questions; all members should participate.
6. Jira setup and detailed assessment guidance will be covered with the supervisor from the following week.
